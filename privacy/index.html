ShoreSense privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. Publisher: Atlas Labs. Contact: atlaslabsdaba@gmail.com.

Short version

ShoreSense has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Location

If you tap “Near me”, the app asks for your approximate location. It is used only on your phone to sort beaches by distance. It is never stored on our side and never sent to us or anyone else. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in your phone settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Favourites and saved data

Your favourite beaches and the last downloaded forecast are stored only on your device, so the app opens fast and works offline. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Network requests

The app downloads public beach data files from our static data site (hosted on GitHub Pages) and map tiles from OpenFreeMap. Like any web server, these hosts may briefly log standard technical information such as your IP address; we do not receive or use it.

Sharing

When you use “Share today”, the app creates an image on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. We do not see what you share.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Safety notice

ShoreSense is not an official safety service. Always follow lifeguards, posted flags and local officials.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
